Assaying bottleneck

Mandi labs lack rapid quality-testing hardware; remote buyers refuse unseen bids.

Persistent cartelization

Local trader cartels collude on online bids; farmers bound to commission agents (arhtiyas) via informal loans are forced to sell offline.

Regulatory fragmentation

Agriculture remains a state subject, causing state-level delays and diluted APMC amendments.

Physical & digital infrastructure lag

Operations stall at mandi gates due to server overloads, rural connectivity drops, and lack of cold-chain integration.

AI quality verification

Mobile camera AI measures size, color, and surface defects; handheld NIR scanners measure moisture and sugar levels directly bound to purchase orders.

Immediate farmgate payouts (T+0)

Delivers instant cash payments upon pickup via fintech/NBFC partners, eliminating arhtiya debt dependence.

Escrow price protection

Locks 100% of buyer funds in escrow prior to dispatch and guarantees prices via forward contracts.

Group supply pooling

Aggregates smallholder crops within a 15 km radius at FPO collection nodes into Full Truckload (FTL) quantities.

Smart transport routing & compliance

Uses APIs and active arbitrage engines to route crops to high-demand markets while automatically generating inter-state e-way bills.
